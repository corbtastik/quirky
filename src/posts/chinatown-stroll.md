We spent an afternoon in one of the most vibrant neighborhoods in
[San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco) 🌉. The energy on
[Grant Avenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grant_Avenue_(San_Francisco)) is
undeniable. Red lanterns string across the sky 🏮. They frame the
[Transamerica Pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transamerica_Pyramid) in
the distance.

We started at the
[Dragon Gate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Gate_(San_Francisco)) 🐉 and
let our feet carry us up the hill ⛰️. Sensory overload, in the best way:

- **The Architecture:** The green-tiled roof of the
  [Sing Chong Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sing_Chong_Building). The
  brick facade of
  [Old St. Mary's Cathedral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_St._Mary%27s_Cathedral_(San_Francisco)).
  The history is written on the walls 🧱✨.
- **The Markets:** We peered into the local grocers 🛒. Fresh produce and dried
  goods, out on the sidewalk 🍇🐟.
- **The Food:** You can't walk these hills without fueling up 🥟🍜. We stopped
  for tea 🍵 and some incredible
  [chow mein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chow_mein). We found a quiet booth
  and escaped the bustle for an hour 😌.

A classic San Francisco day. Blue skies ☀️, steep hills ⛰️, and plenty of
culture packed into a few city blocks 📸.
