After the sun went down,
[San Francisco Chinatown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinatown,_San_Francisco)
turned into a glowing maze. Red lanterns and neon signs 🏮✨. We wandered down
Grant Avenue and soaked up the electric air. Then late-night noodles
before heading home 🍜. Historic architecture against the modern
[Transamerica Pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transamerica_Pyramid).
It makes for an unforgettable evening stroll 🌃.
