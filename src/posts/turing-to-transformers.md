## The Imitation Game

AI does not begin with a microchip. It begins with a question. In 1950 **Alan
Turing** published *Computing Machinery and Intelligence*. He asked: *"Can
machines think?"*

He proposed the **Turing Test**. Put a person in conversation with a machine. If
they cannot tell it from another person, call it intelligent.

> "We can only see a short distance ahead, but we can see plenty there that needs
> to be done."
>
> — **Alan Turing**

---

## The Rollercoaster: Summers and Winters

The **Dartmouth Workshop in 1956** gave the field its name. The optimism ran hot.
Researchers expected a machine as clever as a person inside a generation.

They were wrong.

### The Timeline of Evolution

* **1950s-60s (The Golden Years):** ELIZA held conversations. Other programs played checkers. Logic and rules did all the work.
* **1970s-80s (The AI Winter):** Funding dried up. Rules hit a wall. The real world was too messy for them.
* **1997:** IBM's **Deep Blue** beat Garry Kasparov. Brute force, not thought. Still a milestone.
* **2010s (Deep Learning):** Big data and GPUs arrived together. Neural networks took off. Machines began to see and to hear.

## The Transformer Revolution (2017–Present)

2017 changed everything. Google published *"Attention Is All You Need."* It gave
us the **Transformer architecture**.

Older models read left to right, a word at a time. A Transformer takes the whole
sentence at once. It weighs every word against every other word. That opened the
door to **Large Language Models (LLMs)** like BERT, Claude, and GPT.

AI stopped sorting images. It started *making* poetry, code, and art.

## What's Next? (2026 and Beyond)

It is late 2025 and the chatbot era is peaking. We are moving from AI that
*talks* to AI that *acts*.

### 1. Agentic AI

The next shift is **Agents**. You stop typing a prompt and reading the answer.
You hand over a goal: *"Plan a trip to Tokyo for under $3,000."* The agent
browses. It compares flights. It books the hotel. It fills your calendar. It
loops until the job is done.

### 2. Embodied AI

Brain, meet body. Language models are moving into robots. Nobody hard-codes a
robot to lift a box any more. Vision and language models *teach* it to work in
messy human spaces.

### 3. Reasoners

Models are moving from pattern matching to **System 2 Thinking**. Slow,
deliberate reasoning. A model can pause. It walks through a math problem step by
step. It checks its own work. It fixes the error before it answers.

The history of AI is a history of moving goalposts. A machine learns to do
something. We stop calling it AI and call it software. The next decade will blur
that line until it is gone.
