## Summary

Technologist at MinIO. Before that, architect and technical advisor.

Since 1997: writing software, leading engineering teams, setting enterprise
architecture, and working in technical sales.

The work now is helping companies modernize applications, adopt cloud
platforms, and design data architectures for AI. Still hands-on. Also
workshops, executive briefings, and the writing that makes hard technology
clear.

## Technical expertise

**Application architecture.** Application modernization, microservices, APIs,
event-driven systems, cloud-native development, Java, Spring Boot, TypeScript,
React.

**Cloud and platforms.** Kubernetes, Red Hat OpenShift, AWS, Azure, Google
Cloud, VMware, GitOps, Argo CD, Tekton, Helm.

**Data and AI.** MongoDB Atlas, data modeling, distributed databases, vector
and hybrid search, retrieval-augmented generation, agent memory, OpenShift AI,
KServe, vLLM.

**Advisory.** Discovery workshops, architecture reviews, executive engagement,
demos, webinars, competitive positioning.
